Two-tone display headline — accent-coloured lead phrase followed by white — the signature title treatment of the pitch deck.

```jsx
<Headline lead="ServiceNow was" rest="built for this moment" align="center" />
<Headline lead="Businesses need more" rest="than just intelligence" accent="blue" />
```

- Accent green (default) or blue. Keep it to one accent per headline.
- Tight tracking (-0.025em), line-height .85.
