Pillar card for "X — because Y" arguments (Data / AI / Workflows / Security); pairs a coloured title with a bold white statement.

```jsx
<FeatureCard title="Data" body={<>because AI is only as good as the context it has.</>} media={<img src="…" height={160} />} />
```

- `titleColor` defaults to Blue `#52B8FF`; use Wasabi Green for the hero card.
- `scale` 1 = 1920 slide canvas.
