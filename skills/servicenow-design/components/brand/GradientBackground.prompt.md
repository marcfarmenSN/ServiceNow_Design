Soft-glow brand background from the Figma "ServiceNow Gradients" file; use behind slides, banners, social posts and hero areas.

```jsx
<GradientBackground tone="dark" hue="green" style={{ width: 1920, height: 1080 }}>…</GradientBackground>
```

- `tone`: `dark` (brand marketing, default) · `mid` (owned channels)
- `hue`: `green` (default) · `blue` · `indigo` · `purple` — ordered single → multiple executions
- Pre-rendered PNGs for PPT parity live in `assets/backgrounds/`.
